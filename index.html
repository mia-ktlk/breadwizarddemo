Pistachio Chocolate Cherry Cookies

🏆 2026 Winner 🍪 About 40 cookies Created by Shannon McCullough
▣ Overview ▧ Gallery ▤ Ingredients ☷ Recipe Steps ● Tips & Resources 🛒 Supplies ▣ Get Help

Shannon’s story: My hairdresser guarded this Spumoni Shortbread recipe for years. Her holiday party guests begged for it, and she always said no. It took me two years of asking before she finally shared it with me.

Once a festive little secret, these buttery, colorful cookies are now ready to spread joy all the way to our local food banks.

Batch yieldAbout 40
Total timeAbout 45 min
Oven350°F
Bake time10–12 min

Ingredients What You’ll Need

Important ingredient notes:
Edison all-purpose flour is recommended. Finely chop the maraschino cherries, wrap them in a double layer of paper towels, and squeeze firmly until no more liquid falls. Kirkland Signature roasted, salted pistachios work well. One drop of minty-green food coloring gives a natural holiday color.
IngredientAmount
Unsalted butter, softened1 cup
Green food coloring, minty green1 drop
Almond extract1 tsp
Granulated sugar½ cup / 100g
Edison all-purpose flour2 cups
SaltScant 1 tsp
Maraschino cherries, finely chopped and thoroughly dried1 cup
Roasted, salted pistachios, finely chopped½ cup
Mini chocolate chips½ cup

Instructions Step-by-Step Recipe

1Cream Butter & Flavoring

Beat the softened butter, green food coloring, and almond extract in a large bowl until blended.

2Add Sugar

Add the granulated sugar and beat until thoroughly combined.

3Mix in Flour & Salt

Add the flour and salt. Mix just until the dough comes together; it may still look slightly crumbly.

4Fold in Cherries, Pistachios & Chocolate

Gently fold in the thoroughly dried cherries, finely chopped pistachios, and mini chocolate chips.

5Chill the Dough

Wrap the dough in plastic wrap and refrigerate for 1 hour.

6Preheat & Prepare

Preheat the oven to 350°F. Line a cookie sheet with parchment paper.

7Scoop & Flatten

Use a #60 disher or 1-tablespoon scoop to portion the dough. Roll each portion into a ball, cover it with a small square of parchment, and gently flatten with the bottom of a glass to about ½ inch thick.

8Bake & Cool

Bake for 10–12 minutes, until lightly set. Cool on the cookie sheet for 3–5 minutes, then transfer to a cooling rack and cool completely.

Tips Recipe Notes

Drying the cherries thoroughly is essential—extra moisture can discolor and soften the dough. Keep the pistachios finely chopped for even distribution, and do not skip the one-hour chill.

Equipment Supplies & Equipment

Mixer, large mixing bowl, plastic wrap, parchment paper, cookie sheet, #60 disher or 1-tablespoon scoop, flat-bottomed glass, and cooling rack.

  8. Bake and cool
